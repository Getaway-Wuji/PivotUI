# Eco / Nature

Source: supplied reference board 07, label “Eco / Nature”. Dimension: imagery, subject matter, and visual tone.

## Observed

The example combines forest and water photography with pale and green content areas, an organic-looking mark, and a handwritten circular accent. Natural scenery carries the main theme. These visual cues do not establish that the depicted product or company has environmental credentials, or that green is an appropriate functional status color for another product.

## Suggested use and combinations

Consider a nature-led introduction when the actual subject, audience, and available imagery justify that connection. Outdoor experiences, material stories, or environmental information may benefit, but the task should determine the choice. Pair the image with clear factual copy and restrained controls; simple organic shapes or editorial typography can support it without adding a second competing theme. Existing brand colors may remain intact while photography supplies the natural association.

## Limits, assets, and fallback

Do not infer sustainability claims from a visual direction or invent environmental benefits to match it. Keep textual evidence separate from decoration. Check readability over foliage and water, and preserve useful image content when cropping. Appropriate approved photography is the main dependency; video or animated scenery is optional. Without suitable assets, use a smaller relevant image or a clear text-led section within the existing theme rather than filling the page with unrelated forest imagery.
