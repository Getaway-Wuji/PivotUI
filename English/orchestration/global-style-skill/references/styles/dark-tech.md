# Dark Tech

Source: supplied board 03, label **Dark Tech**. Dimension: dark theme combined with restrained hierarchy and technological imagery. This is a candidate, not a product default.

## Observed evidence

The example combines a nearly black field, a bright headline, quiet navigation, a glowing orb or landscape-like visual, and a compact supporting statistics strip. It is restrained rather than densely neon. The screenshot does not prove theme switching or animated light effects.

## Suggested adoption

Consider this for a focused technology product introduction or an established dark application theme. Define separate page, surface, border, and text roles so regions remain legible without relying entirely on glow. Keep imagery behind or beside information instead of letting it compete with controls.

## Combinations and constraints

Combine with `minimalism`, `swiss`, or `bento-grid` for clear structure. A dark theme can serve many visual directions; it does not require futuristic artwork. Business areas need readable secondary text, distinguishable chart series, clear focus, and stable status meanings. Do not copy large marketing whitespace into a dense operations surface or assume light-theme colors remain suitable after inversion.

## Assets, cost, and fallback

A static atmospheric asset can keep implementation modest. Complex shaders and animated glows are optional additions, not evidence-backed requirements. Fall back to solid layered surfaces and a restrained accent if imagery is unavailable or reduces readability.
