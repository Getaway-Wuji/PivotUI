## Suggested use and combinations

Consider organic framing when a brand introduction, lifestyle feature, or editorial section benefits from a softer relationship between imagery and text. Keep curves subordinate to the message and use a limited family of shapes. Combine the treatment with a clear grid, ordinary controls, or a restrained photographic direction. Existing colors and typography may supply the same sense of softness without adopting the example's exact palette. Let the page owner choose content order and practical section geometry.

## Limits, assets, and fallback

Irregular silhouettes should not shrink useful content space, hide meaningful parts of photographs, or define unpredictable button targets. Check text against every background area it crosses. A suitable photograph and intentional cropping usually matter more than complex masks. Simple SVG or CSS shapes can suffice; elaborate motion is not established. If framing becomes fragile, retain one background curve or use a plain image frame while preserving the calm spacing and hierarchy.
