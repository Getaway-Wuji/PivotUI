# Claymorphism

Source: supplied reference board 06, label “Claymorphism”. Dimension: illustration material and shape language.

## Observed

The example features soft rounded three-dimensional objects, a smiling cloud, pastel scenery, and chunky display lettering. The illustrations suggest a pliable, friendly material. This visual evidence concerns the hero's assets and treatment; it does not establish the behavior or accessibility of inflated-looking controls.

## Suggested use and combinations

Consider a small group of soft objects for a welcoming introduction, onboarding illustration, or playful product explanation when that tone fits the content. Combine the assets with restrained surfaces, clear supporting text, and conventional controls. A coherent illustration can carry the character without applying heavy rounding and soft shadows to every card or input. Keep the visual recommendation scoped to the chosen region and allow component owners to preserve the product's established states and boundaries.

## Limits, assets, and fallback

Soft edges must not make actionable elements indistinguishable from decoration. Check text and focus visibility against the final background rather than assuming pastel colors are readable. A coherent set of rendered assets may require modeling, illustration, or asset selection; animation is not evidenced or required. If suitable assets are unavailable, use one simple flat illustration, a modest rounded accent, or a clean content section. Avoid mixing unrelated three-dimensional asset styles merely to fill space.
