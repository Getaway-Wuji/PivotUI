## Suggested adoption

Consider this for feature overviews, product summaries, or dashboards whose content has genuinely different priorities and footprints. Assign each tile one coherent purpose. Size tiles from information needs, preserving definitions, actions, and useful comparisons. A table or continuous story may need a full-width region instead of forced subdivision.

## Combinations and constraints

Combine with `minimalism`, `swiss`, or a restrained `dark-tech` treatment. Tile geometry does not mandate pastel colors, large radii, or every region becoming a card. Determine reading order before narrow-screen stacking. Avoid isolated decorative metrics, nested cards, and rearrangements that separate related labels and values. Page owners retain responsibility for business grouping.

## Assets, cost, and fallback

Basic layout cost is modest; specialized charts and widgets add their own complexity. Without a suitable image or widget, use truthful text content rather than an invented metric. Fall back to ordered sections or a simple grid while preserving priority and relationships.
