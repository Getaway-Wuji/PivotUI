# Bauhaus

Source: supplied reference board 05, label “Bauhaus”. Dimension: graphic composition and visual direction.

## Observed

The hero combines a heavy headline with a modular arrangement of circles, triangles, arcs, and rectangles. Red, blue, yellow, black, and a pale background separate the geometric areas. Text and illustration occupy distinct regions. This is a particular reference composition, not evidence of a complete historical design system or a working interface.

## Suggested use and combinations

Consider its geometric rhythm for a brand introduction, creative portfolio, editorial opener, or bounded illustration. Borrow the relationships between aligned modules, bold type, and simple shapes rather than imposing the pictured palette. It can combine with a split layout or restrained page typography; choose one dominant geometric composition instead of layering several competing patterns. Let the page owner retain content order and action placement.

## Limits, assets, and fallback

Keep decoration clear of reading and action areas. Colorful shapes must not become ambiguous status indicators or chart categories. Dense business screens may use a small header accent while retaining their established controls. Simple CSS or SVG geometry can be inexpensive; elaborate illustration is optional. When composition competes with the task, fall back to the same alignment and type hierarchy with one geometric accent or none.
