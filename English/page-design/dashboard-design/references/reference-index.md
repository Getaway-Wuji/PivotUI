# Source and Method Summary

This skill distills 37 design images, 30 distinct prompts within them, and eight composition types from one video. S/V IDs come from the research archive and explain where rules originated. Original images, complete prompts, and per-image notes remain production research records; they are neither distributed with the skill nor required for execution.

## Boundaries

- Source titles mix business domains, communication styles, layouts, and themes; they are not mutually exclusive industry templates. See [selection-guide.md](selection-guide.md) for task structure and [visual-recipes.md](visual-recipes.md) for appearance.
- Static images do not demonstrate implemented refresh, keyboard, narrow-screen, or error-recovery behavior. These are added in [interaction-patterns.md](interaction-patterns.md) and [data-and-layout.md](data-and-layout.md).
- Sample values, chart units, targets, and ratios are not calculation authorities. Recheck against actual business data; do not invent findings, targets, or AI recommendations without evidence.
- Only warm-peach glass retains exact source-prompt values as optional starting points. Other color names and shape descriptions are visual syntheses, not measurements or established user brand preferences.
- For exact reconstruction, use the specific image supplied for the current task. Ordinary design tasks can apply this skill's rules directly.
