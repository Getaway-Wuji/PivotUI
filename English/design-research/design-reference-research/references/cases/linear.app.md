## Useful visual relationships

- A near-black page and subtly lighter charcoal panels give product images their own boundary without large shadows. The source lists canvas `#010102`, surface-1 `#0f1011`, surface-2 `#141516`, and hairline `#23252a`.
- Accent `#5e6ad2` is assigned to a small set of actions and emphasis; light text `#f7f8f8` carries the main reading layer. The useful relationship is restrained accent against a neutral surface system.
- The source pairs an 80px/600 display tier with 16px/400 body text, and lists 24px feature-card padding against 96px section spacing. These encode a large page rhythm around relatively compact product presentation.

## Transfer into the target product

For a dark developer-tool marketing page, first choose the product image or demonstration that explains each claim, then frame it with one panel role and a readable boundary. Map the surface relationship to existing neutrals; preserve the actual brand accent. Retain one primary action per meaningful decision region rather than copying every CTA placement. Use existing theme text roles and test the real display copy before adopting any source size.

For an operations interface, borrow only the surface relationship when useful. The page lead still defines metrics, tables, status colors, navigation and return state. Marketing screenshot density does not decide operational density.

## Evidence limits and completion needs

Source pages are explicitly marketing. The source excludes richer product label colors and does not establish full form-error behavior. It gives approximately 36px and 40px in different mobile-heading passages: resolve sizing against content instead of treating either as authoritative. Custom font names are not bundled assets. Large Latin negative tracking is not a Chinese typography rule.

When implementing, complete keyboard, focus, loading, error and narrow-screen behavior for the actual content, and check real contrast. Case values are not library-wide Token defaults or an accessibility pass.
