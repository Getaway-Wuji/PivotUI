# Retrofuturism

Source: supplied board 03, label **Retrofuturism**. Dimension: historical visions of the future expressed through imagery and type. This is a candidate, not a product default.

## Observed evidence

The example pairs a painted science-fiction environment and futuristic city forms with warm terrain, darker teal areas, and an emphatic italic headline. Its identity depends on a nostalgic imagined future. The image does not establish animation, exploration, or a consistent historical period.

## Suggested adoption

Consider this for entertainment, speculative storytelling, exhibitions, or a brand campaign with a relevant narrative. Define the intended era and mood before choosing images and lettering. Give the scene a clear role in the story, while keeping the page's actual offer and action understandable without decoding the illustration.

## Combinations and constraints

Combine with `editorial` for narrative sequencing or `minimalism` for a concentrated scene. Distinguish this from `y2k` material gloss and `cyberpunk` urban intensity; they are optional influences, not required ingredients. Keep scenic detail away from small text and controls. Use ordinary readable typography for longer explanations and working interfaces.

## Assets, cost, and fallback

The main cost is coherent illustration, art direction, and responsive cropping. A static painting can deliver the effect without runtime 3D. If suitable artwork is unavailable, use a limited poster-like graphic and type treatment rather than unrelated futuristic stock imagery.
