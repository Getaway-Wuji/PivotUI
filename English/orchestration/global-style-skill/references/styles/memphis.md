## Suggested use and combinations

Consider this direction for a campaign, creative introduction, playful brand section, or event announcement when the audience and content support that tone. Select a small set of shapes and patterns that share a clear hierarchy. A simple underlying grid and restrained supporting text can keep the composition readable. Pair it with ordinary controls and a clear primary action rather than making every component another collage. Existing product colors remain the starting point unless a change is requested.

## Limits, assets, and fallback

Reserve uncluttered space for text, forms, and actions. Patterns and tilted lettering can reduce scanning speed, especially in dense tasks or translated copy. The cutout and graphic assets require deliberate sourcing and consistent treatment; copying the pictured subject is unnecessary. With limited assets or attention budget, fall back to two restrained decorative shapes around a clear content block, or retain only the playful color accent.
