## Suggested adoption

Consider this for a small, quiet control panel or selected decorative surfaces where soft relief supports the product's character. Keep shadow direction and background relationships coherent. Supplement relief with explicit labels, recognizable control shapes, and distinct active states; do not assign state meaning solely from whether an edge appears raised.

## Combinations and constraints

Combine with `minimalism` for low visual density. Use an ordinary high-contrast control treatment for dense forms, repeated business actions, and critical feedback when soft surfaces weaken boundaries. Text, focus, disabled states, and selected states must remain distinguishable without interpreting subtle shadows. The screenshot provides no evidence that the treatment works across themes.

## Assets, cost, and fallback

Few image assets are needed, but state design and testing across backgrounds can be costly. If relief becomes unclear or crowded, replace it with a solid surface, clear border, and simpler shadow while preserving component dimensions and semantics.
