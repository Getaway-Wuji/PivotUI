## What is included

- Four [metadata shards](references/index.md) identify all 84 source documents with upstream-relative paths, fixed URLs, original-file SHA-256 and byte size. These CSV files and LICENSE are byte-identical between editions.
- Eight locally authored, localized cards: `linear.app`, `stripe`, `notion`, `ibm`, `sap`, `vercel`, `airbnb`, `kraken`. Their useful source values and observations are contained in the cards for offline research. Each links to its fixed source and relevant original line locations.
- A concise selection method and four conditional cross-case patterns. Adaptations are labeled and do not modify shared foundations or impose a brand on every product.

Full source bodies, research transcripts and unneeded assets are not runtime dependencies. A case card is an adaptation, not a byte-identical copy of a company's official specification. Full source texts remain external links for deeper inspection; access to them is optional for the eight cards and necessary before drawing new claims from an indexed-only case.

## Evidence and update policy

The initial audit downloaded 170 text/license files, checked source SHA-256 and byte sizes against a fixed-revision CDN inventory, and inspected eight DESIGN documents. This establishes source integrity and document review only. It does not prove website authenticity, currentness, exact CSS measurement, visual fidelity, accessibility, keyboard behavior, or responsive behavior. The 76 other case records remain `indexed_only` with `unknown` scope/tags.

Do not parse all upstream files as a uniform valid YAML schema: the audited snapshot includes files with no frontmatter and files whose frontmatter cannot be parsed. Inventory values come from the source manifest; case semantics come from document review, not blind field extraction.

For updates, pin a new revision, preserve original provenance, compare changed sources, and review affected case claims, exclusions and source locators before updating their review status. Hash changes detect source drift, not factual accuracy. Maintain English authored instructions first, then semantically synchronize Chinese cards, indexes, guidance and UI metadata. Keep original external files and stable metadata matching between editions. Changes in product tokens belong in that product, not this package.
