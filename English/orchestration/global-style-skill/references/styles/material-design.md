## Suggested use and combinations

Use the example to discuss clear grouping, surface relationships, and a product presentation built from familiar interface fragments. For an existing application, start with its actual component library and theme rather than importing another library because of this label. Marketing product previews can combine layered cards with restrained typography and a simple background. Page and component owners still determine structure, component contracts, and behavior; this reference contributes visual evidence only.

## Limits, assets, and fallback

Shadows alone should not identify interactive controls or critical boundaries. Preserve readable text, distinguish selected and focused states through the product's established conventions, and check actual foreground/background combinations. Device mockups or product captures add asset preparation; they are optional. When depth becomes noisy or expensive, use flat grouped panels with clear separation. Do not invent a complete elevation scale or claim standards conformance from this screenshot.
