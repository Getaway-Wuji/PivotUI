## Suggested adoption

Consider this for fashion, music, creative portfolios, or campaigns where a playful futuristic mood serves the message. Concentrate reflective material in a hero object or display treatment, then use stable ordinary typography for explanatory copy and actions. Choose a small set of coordinated materials instead of making every control metallic.

## Combinations and constraints

Combine with `minimalism` to isolate a dramatic object or `bento-grid` to organize supporting content. Keep `cyberpunk` separate unless its darker urban mood is explicitly desired. Iridescence must not obscure text, status, or focus. Avoid applying decorative dimensional lettering to long labels, forms, or dense business information.

## Assets, cost, and fallback

Convincing reflective assets may require dedicated artwork or rendering. An exported still image is often sufficient; runtime 3D and motion are additional decisions. Without suitable assets, use a restrained static gradient or flat graphic with readable live text rather than simulating chrome everywhere.
