# Cyberpunk

Source: supplied board 03, label **Cyberpunk**. Dimension: urban science-fiction imagery, dark atmosphere, and intense accents. This is a candidate, not a product default.

## Observed evidence

The example uses a densely detailed night city, dark backgrounds, vivid magenta signage, a large emphatic heading, and a technical-looking side region. It suggests a technological urban mood. A still image cannot demonstrate glitch animation, flicker, or interactive instrumentation.

## Suggested adoption

Consider this for games, entertainment, music, or a campaign whose subject supports the atmosphere. Keep one strong scenic focal point, with a stable readable zone for copy and actions. Use vivid accents selectively and separate decorative technical markings from meaningful product data.

## Combinations and constraints

Combine with `dark-tech` to reduce visual noise or `minimalism` to isolate the urban scene. Do not default to this treatment merely because a product involves technology. Avoid animated glitches on reading text, competing neon controls, and invented system readouts. For business tools, preserve familiar status colors and dependable data comparison rather than turning ordinary work into a fictional control room.

## Assets, cost, and fallback

Detailed city imagery and custom graphics can dominate asset cost. Static artwork is sufficient unless motion is separately requested. Use a darker flat backdrop and a limited accent when assets, contrast, or performance make the full scene unsuitable.
