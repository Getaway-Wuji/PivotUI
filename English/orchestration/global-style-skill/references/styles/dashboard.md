# Dashboard preview

Source: supplied reference board 05, label “Dashboard”. Dimension: business archetype and product-preview composition.

## Observed

The example is a marketing hero: promotional text sits beside a preview containing metric cards and charts on light surfaces. It is not a complete operational dashboard. The image does not establish metric definitions, chart correctness, data states, navigation, permissions, or working interactions.

## Suggested use and combinations

Use this reference when presenting an analytics or business product and a dashboard preview can explain its value. Prefer a supplied product capture or a clearly identified illustrative mockup using relevant content. Pair the preview with restrained typography and a simple layout that leaves the main message understandable. For an actual dashboard, retain the dashboard module's ownership of metric priority, data interpretation, charts, and content structure; application shells and data lists keep their own responsibilities. A preview treatment cannot substitute for those methods.

## Limits, assets, and fallback

Do not present invented sample metrics as customer results or real operational evidence. Small charts inside a hero may be illustrative, but essential claims need readable text outside the miniature interface. Product captures require preparation and suitable cropping. Without credible assets or data, use a clearly labelled schematic or concise product summary. For a working business screen, choose the task-appropriate overview or table from its actual requirements.
